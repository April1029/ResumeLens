## Project Description
ResumeLens is a human-centered design project exploring how job seekers, starting with computer science students and early-career candidates, use AI assistance when tailoring their resumes to specific job postings. Our focus is on transparency and human control: understanding what makes AI resume recommendations understandable and actionable, and what causes job seekers to lose control over, or confidence in, how their own qualifications are represented.
